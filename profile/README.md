## About

Macro Evidence is a software organization building open macroeconomic data infrastructure. It applies explicit engineering requirements — provenance, validation, reproducibility, maintainability, and transparent limitations — to make macroeconomic data more coherent, traceable, and researchable.

## Macro Data Observatory

[Macro Data Observatory (MDO)](https://github.com/macro-evidence/macro-data-observatory) is Macro Evidence's flagship and foundational platform: open-source data infrastructure for acquiring, validating, structuring, and maintaining macroeconomic data from authoritative public sources through engineered, reproducible pipelines. MDO is under active development.

## How we work

Development is evidence-paced: scope and architecture are expanded as current evidence justifies them, and non-trivial decisions are documented rather than left implicit.

- [Organization & Platform Charter](https://github.com/macro-evidence/governance/blob/main/ORGANIZATION_CHARTER.md) — mission, platform scope, and long-term direction
- [Governance & Decision-Making](https://github.com/macro-evidence/governance/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) — decision criteria, review, and Architecture Decision Records
- [Documentation Standards](https://github.com/macro-evidence/governance/blob/main/DOCUMENTATION_STANDARDS.md) — documentation ownership and writing conventions
- [Trademarks Policy](https://github.com/macro-evidence/governance/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md) — name, mark, and identity-use policy
